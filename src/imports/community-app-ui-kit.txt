Create a complete mobile UI design system and high-fidelity prototype for a community networking app designed for Indian homemaker entrepreneurs.

The platform helps women who run small home businesses (baking, pickles, tailoring, handmade crafts, homemade meals, beauty services) connect with each other, share knowledge, collaborate, and manage relationships with customers.

The interface must emphasize community, trust, and simplicity, not a competitive marketplace.

Design for mobile iPhone 14 (390x844).

Visual Style Direction

The aesthetic must match the green visual language used in the research insight slides of the first project in the portfolio.

Design should feel:

Warm
Friendly
Supportive
Community-driven
Minimal yet expressive

Use:

• White backgrounds
• Soft green highlights
• Rounded cards
• Soft shadows
• Friendly illustrations of women entrepreneurs

Avoid:

• Corporate fintech aesthetics
• Dark interfaces
• Aggressive colors

The app should feel like a neighborhood women's support network.
