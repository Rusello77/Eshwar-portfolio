Hello, I'm Eshwar.

I'm a UI/UX Designer with a Bachelor's degree in Interaction Design from Strate School of Design. Through experiences at Bosch Global Software Technologies, Strange Avenue and Cropin Technology Solutions, I've worked across enterprise UX, interaction design, UX research, rapid prototyping and AI-assisted design workflows.
